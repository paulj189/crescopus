How disputes work

  • The entries are locked until the dispute is settled. They still count in the totals, flagged “in dispute”.
  • You each give your side. Either of you can ask the CrescoGuide for a neutral summary, which is advisory only.
  • Either of you proposes a resolution; when the other agrees, it's applied to the ledger.
  • If {{ name_of_role[other_role] }} doesn't respond for 14 days, you can close it without agreement. The record stays.
