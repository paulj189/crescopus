About Crescopus

Crescopus is a low-risk way for app developers ("Builders") and growth people ("Growers") to find each other and work together — matched by fit and track record, not the highest bid.

Listing an app or a growth profile is free. When you connect, you get a shared CrescoPlan to agree who needs and offers what, the actions you'll take and how you'll measure success, and a shared CrescoLedger to record costs and revenue that you both confirm.

Crescopus never holds or moves your money, isn't a party to any arrangement between you, and doesn't broker, manage, or guarantee the relationship.

Built by Paul Johnson, an independent developer.

How Crescopus works

Builders are the people who've built an app — developers. Growers are the people who know how to find it an audience — marketing, growth, ASO, that kind of thing. Most people are naturally one or the other, rarely both.

1. Make your card. Builders create a card for each app; Growers create one for themselves. Each card says what you need and what you offer. It's free.

2. Browse and reach out. Filter cards by Needs, Offers and verified members. When one fits, send a short intro — no forms, no numbers.

3. If accepted, you're connected. A CrescoPlan opens for the two of you, along with private messages.

4. Agree a plan together. Either of you can edit the plan: agreed needs and offers, actions with owners and dates, and measures of success. Every save is a new version, and it only counts as agreed when you've both agreed the latest version.

5. Keep a shared ledger. Record costs and revenue in the CrescoLedger. Each entry is confirmed by the other person before it counts, so you both trust the totals.

6. Settle up between you. Any split you agree is paid directly between you. Either of you can end the partnership at any time.

Privacy Policy

Crescopus collects only what's needed to run the platform: your account details, listing and pitch content, and the revenue figures you or connected services (such as RevenueCat) report for calculating partnership splits.

Your data is never sold. It's used solely to operate the matching, pitch and reporting features, and is stored securely with our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Render, Cloudflare).

If you ask the CrescoGuide for a dispute summary, the disputed ledger entries, your plan and what you've both written in the dispute are sent to Claude, an AI service run by Anthropic, to create it. This only happens when one of you asks, after ticking to agree.

You can request a copy of your data, or ask for it to be deleted, at any time by contacting Paul Johnson.
