## What changed and why

The live text still describes the retired model (CrescoPacts, Trial/Formalise,
"records the revenue-share agreements they reach"). The platform now offers:

- free cards (Builders per app, Growers per person) with Needs/Offers;
- a shared **CrescoPlan** both parties edit, with versions and mutual agreement;
- a shared **CrescoLedger** of costs and revenue, each entry confirmed by the other party;
- private messages; ID/GitHub/LinkedIn badges; CSV export.

The main new points the terms need to cover:

1. A CrescoPlan is a shared working plan, **not a contract** prepared by Crescopus, and
   agreeing to a version on the site doesn't on its own create a contract with Crescopus
   (the parties may still create obligations between themselves — kept from the current text).
2. The CrescoLedger is a **record kept by the two parties**. Crescopus doesn't audit,
   verify or certify it, and it isn't a financial statement, tax record or invoice.
3. Any templates, tags or prompts are **guidance, not legal or financial advice**.
4. Users are responsible for what they post (cards, plans, ledger entries, receipt links, messages).
5. Badges show only that a specific check was done, not that a person is trustworthy.
6. Privacy: the new data held (plans and their full history, ledger entries and receipt
   links, messages, badges), how long it's kept, and that the other party in a partnership
   can see shared plan/ledger/message data — including after the partnership ends.

## Proposed Terms & Conditions

**About Crescopus.** Crescopus helps app builders ("Builders") and growth specialists
("Growers") find each other and work together. It provides cards, a shared plan (the
"CrescoPlan"), a shared record of costs and revenue (the "CrescoLedger") and private messages.
Crescopus never holds, moves or guarantees any money.

**Not a party.** Any arrangement between a Builder and a Grower is between them alone.
Crescopus doesn't broker, manage, vet or guarantee it, isn't a party to it, and accepts no
responsibility for the relationship, conduct, promises or outcomes between them — including
non-payment, disputes or how a partnership ends.

**CrescoPlans aren't contracts from us.** A CrescoPlan is a shared working plan written by
the two parties. Crescopus doesn't draft, review or approve it, and agreeing to a version on
Crescopus records that you both agreed to that text; it doesn't create any contract with
Crescopus. Depending on what you actually agree and do, general law (including partnership
law) may still apply between the two of you, however it's described on the platform. If you
want certainty about your legal position, take independent legal advice.

**The CrescoLedger is your own record.** Entries are added and confirmed by the two parties.
Crescopus doesn't check, audit or verify them (unless an entry is clearly marked as coming
from a connected service such as RevenueCat, and even then only reflects what that service
reports). The ledger isn't a financial statement, tax record or invoice. Any split you agree
is settled directly between you.

**No advice.** Tags, prompts, examples and other guidance on Crescopus are there to help you
organise your thinking. They aren't legal, financial or tax advice.

**Your content.** You're responsible for what you post — your card, plan text, ledger entries,
receipt links and messages — and for having the right to share it. Don't post anything
unlawful, misleading, or that belongs to someone else without permission. We may remove
content or suspend accounts that break these terms.

**Badges.** A badge (for example "ID verified" or "GitHub linked") means only that the
specific check was completed. It isn't an endorsement or a guarantee of anyone's
trustworthiness or ability.

**Ending.** Either party can end a partnership at any time. Its plan, plan history and ledger
remain visible, read-only, to both parties. You can close your account at any time.

**Fees.** Crescopus is currently free. If fees are introduced, they'll be announced in advance
and won't apply retroactively. *(Paul: align this with the landing page promise — "any
partnership started while Crescopus is free stays free for its lifetime" — and with your

**As is.** Crescopus is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. To the fullest extent
permitted by law, Crescopus and Paul Johnson accept no liability for any loss, dispute or lost
revenue arising from a partnership, plan, ledger, or use of the platform. Nothing in these
terms limits liability that can't be limited by law.

**Law.** These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England
and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.

## Proposed Privacy Policy

**What we collect.** Your account details (name, email, country); your card content (photo
or logo, summary, about text, Needs and Offers, app details); the CrescoPlans you're part of
and their full version history; CrescoLedger entries, including any receipt links or
references you add; messages between partners; verification badges; and basic technical logs.

**Who can see it.** Your card is public. Plans, plan history, ledger entries and messages are
visible only to the two people in that partnership — and stay visible to both, read-only,
after the partnership ends, because they're a shared record. Your email address isn't shown
to other users.

**How it's used.** Only to run Crescopus: showing cards and filters, matching, plans, ledgers,
messages and notifications. We never sell your data.

**Where it's stored.** With our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database and
images, Render for hosting, Cloudflare for the domain and network). If you connect RevenueCat,
we read revenue totals from it for your ledger.

**How long.** While your account is open. If you close it, your card and profile are deleted,
along with the partnerships you were part of — including their plans, ledgers and messages.
*(Paul: this is how the database works today — deleting a profile deletes its partnerships.
That means a partner loses their shared record when the other person leaves. If you'd rather
the partner keep it, with the leaver's name removed, that's a small database change; then
this sentence would change to match.)*

**Your rights.** Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete
it, or object to how it's used, by contacting Paul Johnson at [CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also
complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
