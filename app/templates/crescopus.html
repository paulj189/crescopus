Grow apps together

Crescopus pairs developers who love building with growth people who love finding an audience — one app, one confluence, shared upside.

I build apps → I grow apps →
The premise

Two skill sets, rarely in one person

Most apps stall for the same reason: the person who built it isn't the person who can sell it. Crescopus exists to put those two people in a room together.

The builder

Made something that works

You'd rather ship features than run ad campaigns. Traction, ASO, marketing — not your craft, and not how you want to spend your time.

  • Keeps ownership of the product, always
  • Sets what a partner can and can't touch
  • Wants a real audience, not another side project
The grower

Knows how to find an audience

Marketing, social, growth loops, deal-making — that's your craft. What you're missing is a product worth putting it behind.

  • Brings a growth plan, not just enthusiasm
  • Earns a share of what they help create
  • Wants a track record they can point to
How it works

A confluence, not a marketplace scramble

Developers describe what they've built and what they're looking for. Growers respond with how they'd take it further. Where there's a fit, both sides agree terms and get to work. Full detail lands when we open the doors.

Built on trust

Fair to both sides, by design

That's what a confluence is for: the point where a builder's risk and a grower's risk both get addressed, not left to chance. Neither side has to take the other's word for it — and unlike a typical marketplace, Crescopus never holds or moves your money, so there's nothing to freeze, delay, or quietly take a cut of.

For builders

You keep what's yours

  • Ownership of the product stays with you, always
  • You set what a partner can and can't touch
  • If you ever sell, it's your call and your terms
For growers

You earn what you build

  • Revenue is tracked and verified, not taken on trust
  • Every confluence builds a track record you can show
  • If the developer sells, you get first right to buy
Where this is headed

Free now. Free for you, later too.

Crescopus doesn't charge anything today — no fee on listings, matching, or the revenue you split with a partner. That won't be true forever: once there's real traction on both sides, we'll introduce a fee on confluences formed from that point on.

Join before that happens, and it never applies to you. Any confluence formed while Crescopus is free stays free for its lifetime — that's not an introductory offer, it's permanent.

The earliest developers and growers also carry a founding member mark on their profile, permanently.
