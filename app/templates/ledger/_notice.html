Before you use the CrescoLedger

The CrescoLedger is a shared record to help you both keep track of costs, time and revenue, and to support your discussions and negotiations.

Confirming an entry only means you agree it's an accurate record. It doesn't create any obligation to pay, repay, share or recover anything.

Any obligations between you, including payments, recovering costs and sharing revenue, are agreed and dealt with directly between the Builder and the Grower, outside the scope of Crescopus.

Crescopus doesn't hold or move money, isn't a party to any arrangement between you, and doesn't check or guarantee the figures.
