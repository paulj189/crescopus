About Crescopus

Crescopus is a low-risk way for app developers ("Builders") and growth people ("Growers") to find each other and explore working together — matched by fit and track record, not the highest bid.

Every relationship starts as a Trial CrescoPact: no commitment, no numbers, just an introduction and a chat. If it's a fit, either side can propose to Formalise it. Revenue-sharing terms ("CrescoShares") are entirely optional and only come into play after that, whenever you're both ready.

Crescopus never holds or moves your money, isn't a party to any CrescoPact, and doesn't broker, manage, or guarantee the relationship between you.

Built by Paul Johnson, an independent developer.

How Crescopus works

Builders are the people who've built an app — developers. Growers are the people who know how to find it an audience — marketing, growth, ASO, that kind of thing. Most people are naturally one or the other, rarely both.

1. A Builder lists an app. Just the basics — no revenue details required to get started.

2. Either side reaches out. A Grower browsing listings can send a short message about a app that interests them, or a Builder browsing Growers can do the same. No forms, no numbers — just a quick intro.

3. If accepted, you're connected. This is a Trial CrescoPact: email addresses are shared and an in-app chat opens. Nothing's been agreed yet — it's just a conversation.

4. Talk it through. A Builder can be chatting with several Growers on the same app at once. Either side can end a Trial at any point if it's not a fit — that's recorded, but it's a normal part of exploring, not a failure.

5. If it clicks, propose to Formalise. This is a mutual step that signals you're committing to work together on this app specifically.

6. Revenue terms are optional, and later. Once formalised, you can set up a revenue split ("CrescoShares") whenever you're both ready — it's never required to start exploring.
