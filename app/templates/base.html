About Crescopus

Crescopus pairs developers who've built apps with monetisers who know how to grow them — matched by fit and track record, not the highest bid, and structured as an ongoing revenue-share partnership rather than a one-off sale.

We call that partnership a CrescoPact. It isn't a legal contract — it's a clear, mutual starting point. Both sides agree terms and set CrescoShares to represent the stake each of you holds, then try the partnership out. If it works, you're free to formalise it separately; if it doesn't, either side can walk away without needing a lawyer to get involved first. A CrescoPact can cover one or several of an app's revenue streams.

Crescopus never holds or moves your money. Revenue is reported and the split is calculated on-platform; payment happens directly between the two parties.

Built by Paul Johnson, an independent developer.

Privacy Policy

Crescopus collects only what's needed to run the platform: your account details, listing and proposal content, and the revenue figures you or connected services (such as RevenueCat) report for calculating partnership splits.

Your data is never sold. It's used solely to operate the matching, proposal and reporting features, and is stored securely with our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Render, Cloudflare).

You can request a copy of your data, or ask for it to be deleted, at any time by contacting Paul Johnson.
